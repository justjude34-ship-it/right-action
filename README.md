# Right Action

A personal-year companion inspired by Juno Jordan's timing method in *Your Right Action Number*.

Quiet, practical, grown-up. Not affiliated with DeVorss or the Jordan estate. Not a copy of the book. Not fortune-telling. Not therapy.

Local-only PWA. Buy once, own forever.

## What it does
- Finds your Personal Year, Month and Day from birth month and day
- Gives the year's right action — what this chapter is for
- Month-by-month planner inside that year
- Nine-year cycle map
- Saved people on this phone only

Open on your phone and Add to Home Screen.

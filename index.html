How it works

A timing lens, not a verdict

Juno Jordan (1884–1984) spent fifty years counselling with numbers. Your Right Action Number (DeVorss, 1979) is her book of Personal Year cycles — what each of the nine years is for, and how to meet every month inside it.

This app uses that same public method. The wording here is original. It is not a reprint of her book, and it is not affiliated with DeVorss & Company or the Jordan estate.

The sums

Personal Year = birth month + birth day + calendar year, reduced to 1–9.

Personal Month = Personal Year + calendar month, reduced to 1–9.

Personal Day = Personal Month + calendar day, reduced to 1–9.

The year runs with the calendar: 1 January to 31 December. Master numbers are reduced; the Personal Year is always a single digit.

On your phone

In the browser menu choose Add to Home Screen. After that it opens like an app and works offline. Nothing is uploaded. Not therapy. Not fate. You stay in the driver’s seat.
